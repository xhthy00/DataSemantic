## 通用关联骨架

```sql
tb_score sc
JOIN tb_exam e ON sc.exam_id = e.id
LEFT JOIN tb_exam_batch eb ON e.exam_batch_id = eb.id
JOIN tb_school sch ON sc.school_id = sch.id
-- 学生需要时：sc.student_id = st.id
-- 小题：sd.exam_id = sc.exam_id AND sd.student_id = sc.student_id
-- 题目：sd.question_id = eq.id（并校验试卷一致）
-- 知识：eq.id = eqk.question_id；eqk.knowledge_id = k.id
```

成绩与总览关联还必须带批次，例如 ov.anon_stu_id=sc.student_id AND ov.exam_batch_id=e.exam_batch_id；若旧数据只有批次名，先规范名称并验证唯一性。上述为逻辑骨架，实际使用前核对当前字段和空值。
